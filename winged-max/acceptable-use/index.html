Acceptable Use Policy

Published 12 November 2026 · Applies to Winged Max only

Winged Max is intended for adults using their own authorized accounts on third-party dating apps to explore mutual connections and arrange consensual, lawful dates. You remain responsible for your choices, messages, and conduct, including anything the assistant suggests or drafts.

Prohibited conduct

Do not use Winged Max to:

  • Target minors, misrepresent your age or identity, or use the service if you are under 18.
  • Harass, threaten, stalk, exploit, or pressure anyone, or continue contacting someone after they ask you to stop.
  • Impersonate another person, scam others, solicit money under false pretenses, or send deceptive or abusive messages.
  • Create, distribute, or facilitate unlawful sexual content or activity, sexual exploitation, trafficking, or any other illegal conduct.
  • Access accounts without permission, reveal another person's private information, or evade security or access controls.
  • Use the service for scraping, mass messaging, spam, or automation that violates applicable law or a third-party app's rules.
  • Attack Winged Max, distribute malware, or interfere with the service or other users.

Reports and response

Report suspected misuse or safety concerns to support@wingedapp.com with enough context for us to review. Do not email card details or unnecessary sensitive information. We may investigate and take proportionate action, including limiting access, when we have a reasonable basis to believe this policy has been breached. We may refer suspected unlawful activity to appropriate authorities when legally required or permitted.
