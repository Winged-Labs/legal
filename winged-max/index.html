Winged Max policies

Winged Max is a macOS assistant for adults who use their own accounts on existing dating apps. It helps them understand their preferences, consider potential matches, draft messages, and plan in-person dates.

US pilot in preparation. Winged Max subscriptions are not yet available for live purchase. The final price and first charge date will be shown before a customer starts a trial.
