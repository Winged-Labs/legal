Law-Enforcement Request Protocol

Published 12 November 2026 · Applies to Winged Max only

This page explains how an authority may submit a request for information held by Winged Oy about Winged Max. Winged Oy is organized in Finland. Our postal address is Kalasatamankatu 17 A 23, 00580 Helsinki, Finland. This protocol does not cover the separate Winged member-matching app.

Contact

Send official requests to support@wingedapp.com with “Winged Max law-enforcement request” in the subject line. This is the active intake address for Winged Max. Please do not send unnecessary sensitive personal information in an unsecured email.

Information to include

Please identify your agency, officer or contact, official reply address, jurisdiction, the specific Winged Max account or other identifier, the records sought, the relevant date range, the legal basis and process supporting the request, and any applicable deadline. We may seek verification of the request's authenticity and ask for clarification or narrower scope.

Review and disclosure

Winged Oy reviews the request's authenticity, legal validity, jurisdiction, and scope. We disclose information only as required or permitted by applicable law. A preservation request does not itself authorize disclosure. Where appropriate, we may consult counsel or challenge a request.

Emergencies

If you believe there is an imminent threat of death or serious physical harm, mark the request clearly as an emergency, explain the facts supporting urgency, and include a direct callback number. This email address is not an emergency hotline and no 24-hour response is promised. Emergency requests are still assessed under applicable law.

Records and user notice

We record requests and responses according to our internal procedures. Whether a user is notified depends on applicable law and the circumstances of the request. This page does not create a right to access data or change legal rights and obligations.
