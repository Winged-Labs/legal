4. Trial and recurring billing

You may download Winged Max, sign in, and complete local onboarding without starting a subscription. Before the first cloud-powered action, you will be offered a Stripe-hosted Checkout page. If you choose to start a subscription, Checkout will show the monthly price, any applicable taxes, the seven-day free trial, and the first charge date before you confirm.

A card is required to start the seven-day trial. The subscription charge at signup is $0. A temporary card-verification authorization may appear; it is not the subscription charge. Unless you cancel before the trial ends, Stripe will attempt the first charge when the trial ends, and the subscription will then renew monthly until canceled. There are no chat credits or per-message charges.

5. Cancellation and access

You may cancel through the subscription-management option in Winged Max, which opens Stripe's customer portal, or request help at support@wingedapp.com. Cancel before the trial ends to avoid the first subscription charge. For an active paid subscription, cancellation normally stops the next renewal and access continues through the paid period. If a payment fails, access may be limited while payment is resolved. Your Stripe customer portal and account status will show your subscription's current dates and state.
